Pasta Recipe

Description

Tender corkscrew pasta is cloaked in a creamy mushroom sauce sporting flavors of three
cheeses.

Ingredients

  • Campbell's® Condensed Cream of Mushroom Soup
  • shredded two-cheese blend
  • grated Parmesan cheese
  • milk
  • ground black pepper
  • cooked corkscrew-shaped pasta

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Mix soup, cheeses, milk and black pepper in 1 1/2-qt. casserole. Stir in pasta
  3. Bake at 400 degrees F. for 20 min. or until hot.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
