pancakes recipe

Description

This is an easy recipe to start your day off right with a tasty meal!

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Milk and Oil
  • Eggs

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl; make a well in the
    center. Pour in milk, oil, and egg; mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat. Pour or scoop
    about ¼ cup batter per pancake onto the griddle; cook until bubbles form and
    the edges are dry, 1 to 2 minutes. Flip; cook until browned on the other side.
    Repeat with remaining batter.
  4. Serve hot and enjoy!
